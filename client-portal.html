Portal Workflow

Sign up, log in, and review posted invoices.

Each client sees only the invoice documents stored in their own Firebase record.

What Clients Can Do
  • Create an account with email and password
  • Log in from any device
  • Review posted invoice amounts, statuses, and due dates
How You Manage It

After a client account exists, you add invoice documents in Firebase under that client UID.

Portal Access

Log in to your invoice portal

Existing clients can log in here. New clients can create an account first, then you can post invoices to their record.

Firebase setup still needed. Add your Firebase project values in script/firebase-config.js, enable Email/Password auth, and create Firestore before client login will work.
For You As The Site Owner

After a client signs up, find their UID in Firebase Authentication and post invoice documents to clients/{uid}/invoices.
